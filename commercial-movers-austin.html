Office move checklist

Keeping your business open through the move

A commercial move succeeds or fails on planning that happens weeks before the truck arrives.

  • Confirm both buildings’ rules. Ask each landlord for their certificate-of-insurance requirements, freight elevator booking and loading dock hours.
  • Make a floor plan. Number every desk, room and storage area on the new layout and label boxes and furniture to match. Hand the crew a copy.
  • Plan IT separately. Back up data, and have your IT provider disconnect and reconnect servers, networks and phones. Label cables before they are unplugged.
  • Update your address everywhere. USPS, your Google Business Profile, website, vendors, insurance, licenses and payroll. Forward phones before moving day.

Common questions

Can a commercial move happen after hours or on a weekend?+

Commercial moves are scheduled around your business. Tell us when you can and can’t be closed, and we’ll plan the timing in the proposal.

What do landlords usually require from a mover?+

Most commercial buildings ask for a certificate of insurance naming the landlord, a reserved freight elevator and loading dock time, and floor or wall protection in common areas.

How do we keep downtime short?+

Pack non-essential files and storage early, label everything to a floor plan, and have IT ready to reconnect the moment workstations are placed.
