The part of moving everyone forgets

Most people unpack the kitchen and bedroom, then live around boxes for weeks. Unpacking help turns that into a day.

What unpacking can include

  • Unpacking boxes into the right rooms, cabinets and closets
  • Reassembling beds, tables and other furniture
  • Placing furniture according to your floor plan
  • Collecting packing paper and empty boxes

Pick the rooms that matter most

Kitchens take the longest to unpack and make the biggest difference. Many people have the crew handle the kitchen and bedrooms, then finish the rest themselves.

Make it smooth

Label boxes with the room and a short list of contents, and decide where major pieces go before moving day. It's faster when the crew doesn't have to guess.
