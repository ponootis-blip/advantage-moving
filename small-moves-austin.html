When a small move makes sense

Not every move fills a truck. Small moves are common when you're moving into a furnished place, splitting a household, clearing a storage unit or bringing home a few big purchases.

Typical small moves

  • Studios and small apartments
  • A single room or a home office
  • Moving items out of, or into, a storage unit
  • A sofa, dresser or appliance between homes

Small doesn't mean simple

A single sleeper sofa up a third-floor walk-up can be harder than a dozen boxes on the ground floor. Tell us exactly what's moving and how the access looks at both ends, and we'll plan the right crew.

Make it efficient

Have everything in one place, boxes packed and taped, and parking cleared before the crew arrives. Small moves go fastest when nothing needs to be gathered on the day.
