Texas moving FAQs

Before the long haul.

Is an Austin-to-Dallas move intrastate?+

Yes. A move that begins and ends within Texas is an intrastate move, even when it covers several hundred miles.

How is a Texas move priced?+

Distance, shipment size, access, labor, timing and optional services all affect the estimate.

How do I verify a Texas mover?+
