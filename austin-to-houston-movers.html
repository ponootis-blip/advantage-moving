Planning a move from Austin to Houston

Houston is about 165 miles from Austin by road—roughly 2½–3 hours of driving, depending on traffic and where you start and finish. The usual route is US 290 or SH 71 to I-10. Distance is only part of the plan: access at both homes, the size of the move and building rules decide how long the day really takes.

Advantage Moving is a full-service, family-owned mover that has handled moves across Texas since 2001. One coordinated crew packs, protects, loads, drives and places your belongings, so nothing is handed off to strangers halfway.

What’s different about moving to Houston

Houston’s high-rises and apartment towers usually require a reserved freight elevator, a loading-dock time and a certificate of insurance. Hurricane season runs from June through November, so leave a little flexibility in late-summer schedules, and protect wood furniture and electronics from Gulf Coast humidity.

Your rights on a Texas move

Because the move stays inside Texas, it’s regulated by the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles, not the federal FMCSA. That means an active TxDMV certificate (ours is #000572890B), a written proposal before anything is loaded, liability agreed in advance and a signed contract at delivery. Making an Austin to Houston move easy

A longer move rewards a little extra planning. These steps keep the day predictable.

  • Book early for peak dates. Summer and month-end dates fill first on every Texas route.
  • Line up access at both ends. Elevator and dock reservations, gate codes and parking for a full-size truck at the destination.
  • Keep essentials with you. Medication, documents, keys, chargers and valuables ride in your car, not the truck.
  • Be reachable on delivery. Someone should be at the new home to direct the crew, note any damage and sign the contract.

Common questions

How long does an Austin to Houston move take?+

The drive itself is roughly 2½–3 hours. Total time depends on the size of your home and the access at both ends, and your written proposal will set out the pickup and delivery schedule.

Who regulates a move from Austin to Houston?+

Because it stays inside Texas, the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles regulates it. Your mover must hold an active TxDMV certificate and give you a written proposal before loading.

Do you move to Houston suburbs?+

Yes. We move anywhere in Texas, including suburbs such as Katy, Sugar Land and The Woodlands.

What should I tell you for an accurate quote?+

Both addresses, the size of the move, stairs or elevators at each end, any heavy or delicate items and your target dates.
