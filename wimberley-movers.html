Moving in Wimberley and Woodcreek

Wimberley and neighboring Woodcreek sit in the Hays County Hill Country along RR 12, where Cypress Creek meets the Blanco River. Homes range from in-town cottages to houses on wooded hillsides and river lots.

Narrow roads and hillsides

Winding roads, steep driveways and limited turnaround space are common. Let us know what the road in looks like so we can bring the right truck.

Low-water crossings

Some neighborhoods are reached by crossings that close during heavy rain. If storms are forecast, we'll talk through timing and routes with you.

Small-town logistics

Wimberley's square and main roads get busy on market days and weekends. We plan arrival times to avoid the crowds where we can.
