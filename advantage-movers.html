Our story

Built one careful move at a time.

Advantage Moving is locally owned and operated by the Cousins family. Since 2001, the company has grown by helping neighbors move homes, apartments, offices, restaurants, pianos and belongings across Central Texas and the state.

The principle is simple: arrive prepared, set reasonable expectations, handle people and property with care, and keep the pricing straightforward.

Based south of Austin

Our office at 2462 Satterwhite Road in Buda places us close to South Austin, Buda, Kyle, Dripping Springs and the communities along the I-35 corridor.

Licensed and accountable

Advantage Moving is licensed and insured for Texas moving. Our TXDMV certificate number is 000572890B. Texas consumers should verify a mover’s active certificate and get proposal and contract terms in writing.

What we do

Our work includes local residential moving, Texas intrastate moving, commercial relocation, packing and specialty-item moving.
