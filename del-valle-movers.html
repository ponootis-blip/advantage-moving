Moving in Del Valle

Del Valle covers much of southeast Travis County around SH 71 and SH 130, near Austin-Bergstrom International Airport and Circuit of the Americas. New neighborhoods and apartment communities have brought a wave of first-time moves.

New-build neighborhoods

Moving into a new home often means unfinished landscaping, construction traffic and builder rules about where trucks can park. Check with the builder before moving day.

Event traffic

Big events at Circuit of the Americas and airport traffic can slow nearby roads. Tell us your date and we'll check timing.

Apartments

Many new apartment communities set move-in hours and elevator or gate reservations. See apartment moving.
