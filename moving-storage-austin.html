Storage coordination for in-between moves

Moves don't always happen on one clean date. A closing slips, a renovation runs long or a lease ends a week early. Instead of rushing, we coordinate short-term storage so your belongings are packed, protected and waiting until the new home is ready.

How it works

  1. Tell us both dates: when you need to be out and when you can move in.

  2. We plan the move-out and the delivery as two parts of the same job.

  3. Your belongings go into storage, then come to the new home when you're ready.

What to keep out of storage

  • Medication, documents and valuables
  • Anything you'll need in the meantime: seasonal clothes, work gear, school items
  • Perishable, flammable or hazardous items

Get it in writing

Storage adds a second moving day. Ask how storage, access and delivery will be handled, and make sure every part is in your written proposal.
