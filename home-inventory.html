Why an inventory helps

  • A more accurate quote: a clear list of furniture and boxes is the single most useful thing you can share when you request an estimate.
  • Better planning: it shows how big the move really is, room by room.
  • Peace of mind: in Texas, an inventory signed before and after the move is useful if you ever need to file a claim for loss or damage. See your rights on a Texas move.

Tips for a useful list

  • Start with big furniture, then add boxes as you pack
  • Mark fragile and valuable items so they get extra care
  • Photograph valuable pieces from a few angles
  • Note anything that comes apart, like bed frames and tables

Common questions

Inventory questions

Do I need an inventory for my move?+

It isn’t required, but it makes your quote more accurate and is useful in Texas if you ever need to file a damage claim.

How is the weight estimated?+

With the common rule of thumb of about 7 pounds per cubic foot of household goods. Real weights vary.

Is my list saved?+

Yes, in this browser only. Download a CSV or print it to keep a copy.
