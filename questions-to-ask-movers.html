Moving guide

Ten questions to ask before you hire a mover.

The right questions sort the professionals from the risks in a single phone call. Here's what to ask—and the answers to listen for.

Ask us these questions

We're happy to answer every one.

1. What's your TxDMV certificate number?

Every household mover in Texas needs an active certificate. Check it in the state's Truck Stop database. Ours is #000572890B.

2. Will I get a written proposal before you load?

The answer must be yes—Texas requires it. Be wary of anyone who only quotes by phone.

3. Is the price binding or not-to-exceed?

Binding is the exact price; not-to-exceed is the maximum. Either is fine, as long as you know which one you have.

4. What could change the final price?

Listen for clear answers about time, stairs, access and extra services—not vague reassurance.

5. What's your liability for damage, and can I increase it?

Texas lets you and the mover agree on liability before the move. Some agreements limit it to 60 cents per pound per item. That isn't insurance, so ask about options and consider your own coverage.

6. Who will actually do my move?

Ask whether the company's own crew handles the job from start to finish.

7. How do you protect my home and furniture?

Pads, wrap, floor and doorway protection should be standard answers.

8. What happens if my dates change?

Closings slip. Ask how changes are handled and whether storage can be coordinated.

9. How do I reach you on moving day?

You want a real person on the phone, not a call center. Our phone is answered daily, 8am to 5pm.

10. How do claims work if something is damaged?

In Texas you file a written claim within 90 days of delivery, and TxDMV offers free mediation if you and the mover can't agree. See how Texas protects your move.

Red flags

Walk away if you see these.

  • No TxDMV number, or an inactive one
  • No written proposal
  • Pressure to sign quickly
  • Unmarked or rental trucks
  • A price far below everyone else's

Common questions

Hiring a mover

How do I check a mover's license in Texas?+

Ask for the TxDMV certificate number and look it up in TxDMV's Truck Stop database. It should say Active.

Where can I file a complaint about a mover?+

With TxDMV online, by calling 1-888-368-4689 or by emailing TruckStop@TxDMV.gov. Moves that cross state lines are handled by the federal FMCSA instead.

Should I pay a large deposit up front?+

Read the written proposal: it should state when payment is due and how. Be cautious with any mover that won't put payment terms in writing.
