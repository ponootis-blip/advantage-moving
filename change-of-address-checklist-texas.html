Required by Texas

Driver license or ID: within 30 days

Texas requires you to change the address on your driver license or ID card within 30 days of moving. You can do it online, by mail or in person. Source: Texas DPS ↗.

Vehicle registration

Update the address on your vehicle registration with the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles so renewal notices reach you. A USPS change of address does not update TxDMV records. Visit TxDMV ↗.

Voter registration

Update your voter registration address so you can vote at your new polling place. Start at VoteTexas.gov ↗.

Mail and accounts

  • File a change of address with USPS ↗
  • Banks, credit cards and insurance (home, auto and health)
  • Employer and payroll
  • Subscriptions, deliveries and online stores
  • Doctors, dentists, pharmacy and veterinarian
  • Schools and childcare

Utilities in the Austin area

  • Electric: inside the City of Austin, service is through Austin Energy; elsewhere, your provider depends on the address—ask your landlord, builder or the previous owner
  • Water, wastewater and trash: usually your city or local utility district
  • Gas: if the home has gas service, schedule a start date
  • Internet: book installation early; appointments can be weeks out

Schedule shut-off at the old home for the day after you move out, and start-up at the new home for the day before you arrive.

Key deadlines

For moves within Texas.

  • Driver license: within 30 days
  • Vehicle registration: update address
  • Voter registration: before the next election
  • USPS: about two weeks before
  • Utilities: 1–2 weeks before

Common questions

Change-of-address questions

How long do I have to change my driver license address in Texas?+

Thirty days after moving, according to the Texas Department of Public Safety. You can update it online, by mail or in person.

Does a USPS change of address update my vehicle registration?+

No. Update your vehicle registration address with the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles separately.

When should I set up utilities at my new home?+

Schedule them one to two weeks before your move, with service starting the day before you arrive.
