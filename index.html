What kinds of moves do you handle?+

We handle local residential moves, Texas intrastate moves, office and commercial relocations, packing, short-term storage and specialty items including pianos and antiques.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes. Advantage Moving is licensed and insured. Our Texas Department of Motor Vehicles number is 000572890B.

How is my estimate calculated?+

We consider the size of your move, access at both locations, travel distance, crew needs and optional services. Call or send your details for a personalized estimate.

Do you move outside Austin?+

Yes. We serve Greater Austin and Central Texas, and we move customers anywhere within the state of Texas.

Can you help with packing?+

Yes. Our crews arrive with moving essentials and can help with packing, shrink-wrapping and protecting floors, walls and delicate belongings.
