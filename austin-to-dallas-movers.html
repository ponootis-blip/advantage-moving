Planning a move from Austin to Dallas–Fort Worth

Dallas–Fort Worth is about 195 miles from Austin by road—about 3–3½ hours of driving, depending on traffic and where you start and finish. The usual route is I-35 north (splitting to I-35E for Dallas and I-35W for Fort Worth). Distance is only part of the plan: access at both homes, the size of the move and building rules decide how long the day really takes.

Advantage Moving is a full-service, family-owned mover that has handled moves across Texas since 2001. One coordinated crew packs, protects, loads, drives and places your belongings, so nothing is handed off to strangers halfway.

What’s different about moving to Dallas–Fort Worth

The Metroplex is spread out: confirm whether you’re headed to Dallas, Fort Worth or a suburb, since that changes the route and timing. Uptown and downtown buildings commonly require dock reservations and insurance certificates, and many suburbs have HOA move-hour rules.

Neighborhoods and suburbs

We move to Dallas–Fort Worth and its suburbs, including Plano, Frisco, Arlington, Irving and McKinney. Tell us the exact address when you request your quote.

Your rights on a Texas move

Because the move stays inside Texas, it’s regulated by the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles, not the federal FMCSA. That means an active TxDMV certificate (ours is #000572890B), a written proposal before anything is loaded, liability agreed in advance and a signed contract at delivery. Making an Austin to Dallas–Fort Worth move easy

A longer move rewards a little extra planning. These steps keep the day predictable.

  • Book early for peak dates. Summer and month-end dates fill first on every Texas route.
  • Line up access at both ends. Elevator and dock reservations, gate codes and parking for a full-size truck at the destination.
  • Keep essentials with you. Medication, documents, keys, chargers and valuables ride in your car, not the truck.
  • Be reachable on delivery. Someone should be at the new home to direct the crew, note any damage and sign the contract.

Common questions

How long does an Austin to Dallas–Fort Worth move take?+

The drive itself is about 3–3½ hours. Total time depends on the size of your home and the access at both ends, and your written proposal will set out the pickup and delivery schedule.

Who regulates a move from Austin to Dallas–Fort Worth?+

Because it stays inside Texas, the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles regulates it. Your mover must hold an active TxDMV certificate and give you a written proposal before loading.

Do you move to Dallas–Fort Worth suburbs?+

Yes. We move anywhere in Texas, including suburbs such as Plano, Frisco and Arlington.

What should I tell you for an accurate quote?+

Both addresses, the size of the move, stairs or elevators at each end, any heavy or delicate items and your target dates.
