Pets ride with you

Moving trucks aren’t climate-controlled or safe for animals. Plan to move pets in your own car, in a secured carrier, with water and their own kit.

Moving day is stressful for pets

Open doors, strangers and noise can frighten pets or let them slip out. Keep them in a closed, quiet room with a sign on the door—or with a friend or sitter—until the crew is finished.

Texas heat

A parked car can become dangerous for pets in minutes on a Texas day. Never leave pets in the car, schedule long drives for cooler hours, and keep water handy.

Plants

Plants do best in your car, too. A closed truck gets very hot. Water lightly a few days before, shade them from direct sun through the windows, and consider giving away anything too large to move.

Moving with kids too? See moving with kids.

Pet travel kit

Keep it in your car.

  • Food and water
  • Bowls and leash
  • Litter or waste bags
  • Medications
  • Vet records
  • A familiar blanket

Common questions

Pet and plant questions

Can my pets ride in the moving truck?+

No. Pets should travel with you in your own car, in a secured carrier, with water and their essentials.

Can movers move my houseplants?+

Plants are best moved in your own car, because a closed moving truck can get very hot—especially in Texas.

Where should my pet be on moving day?+

In a closed, quiet room with a sign on the door, or with a friend or sitter, until the crew is finished.
