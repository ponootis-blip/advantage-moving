How to check us out

A family business should be easy to verify. Here’s where to look before you book—with us or anyone else.

  • Our state license. Look up TxDMV certificate #000572890B in the state’s Truck Stop database and confirm it is active. TxDMV mover lookup ↗
  • Complaint history. TxDMV’s Enforcement Division can tell you about any mover’s complaint history at 1-888-368-4689, and the Better Business Bureau keeps its own records.
  • Reviews from real customers. Read recent reviews on Google and other review sites, not just the ones a company chooses to show.
  • Everything in writing. Texas requires a written proposal before anything is loaded. Read it, ask questions, and don’t rely on verbal promises.
