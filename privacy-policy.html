Privacy policy

Plain language, no hidden fine print.

Short version: your details are used to quote and schedule your move—nothing else.

What we collect

When you request a quote, we receive your name, phone number, email address, move locations and date, and any details you share about your move.

How we use it

Only to prepare your estimate, contact you about your move, and provide our services. We do not sell or rent your personal information. Online quote requests are processed by Formspree so they can be stored in the company form inbox and delivered to the Advantage Moving team by email.

Unfinished quote requests

If you start the quote form and leave before sending it, your answers are saved only in your own browser so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us until you press send, and they’re removed when you send the form, click “Start over”, or after 30 days.

Service provider and retention

Formspree processes online quote requests on our behalf. The company periodically exports or deletes submissions according to its operating needs. Please do not include payment-card, Social Security, medical, or other highly sensitive information in a quote request.

Questions or deletion requests

Call us at (512) 443-6141 and we will answer your question—or delete your quote details on request.
