Local knowledge matters

A mover built for life south of Lady Bird Lake.

South Austin is not one kind of neighborhood, and no two moves here run quite the same. A second-floor walk-up off South Congress needs a different plan than a family home near Circle C. A bungalow in Bouldin Creek can mean narrow access and careful floor protection; a new community near Onion Creek may mean gates, loading windows and a longer carry.

Advantage Moving brings the planning, equipment and experienced crew to handle those details before they turn into delays. We serve homeowners, renters and businesses throughout South Austin, including ZIP codes 78704, 78745, 78748, 78749 and 78744.
