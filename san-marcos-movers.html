Full-service movers for San Marcos

We pack, protect, load, move and set up, and we’re at our best on the moves that need experience: walk-up apartments, tight stairwells, heavy furniture and long carries from parking to the door.

Apartments and student housing

Many San Marcos apartment communities set move-in hours, require reservations for elevators or gates, and get busy around the start and end of semesters and leases. Ask the leasing office for their rules early and share them with us.

Homes along the corridor

Moving in San Marcos

Tips for a smooth San Marcos move

A few local details make a big difference on moving day.

  • Check move-in rules. Leasing offices often set move hours and reserve elevators or loading areas—get them in writing.
  • Avoid peak weekends if you can. Semester and lease turnovers make some weekends far busier than others.
  • Measure for big pieces. Apartment stairwells and doorways can be tight for sectionals and box springs.
  • Beat the heat. Morning starts are easier on everyone during a Central Texas summer.
