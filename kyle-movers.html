Moving in Kyle

What to plan for in Kyle

Kyle has grown fast, and that growth shapes moving day: new subdivisions, apartment communities and heavy I-35 traffic.

  • Apartment move windows. Many communities set move-in hours and ask for elevator or gate access in advance. Get the rules from the office early.
  • HOA rules in new neighborhoods. Check limits on truck parking and move times so the crew isn’t turned away at the gate.
  • Plan around I-35. If you’re moving between Kyle and Austin, leave room in the schedule for rush-hour traffic.
  • Measure for big furniture. Newer floor plans can have tight stair turns. Note them so the right crew size and equipment arrive.
