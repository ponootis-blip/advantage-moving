Why you need one

The first night in a new home is when you can’t find the phone charger, the shower curtain or the coffee. A first-night box solves that: everything you need for one night and morning, in one place.

Pack it last, open it first

Pack it the night before, label it OPEN FIRST on every side, and keep it with you in your car. Then nothing important is buried in the truck.

Austin tip

In a Texas summer, keep medication, electronics and anything heat-sensitive in an air-conditioned car rather than a hot truck.

For everything else on moving day, see the moving day guide.

Remember

Keep these with you.

  • Medication
  • IDs and documents
  • Keys
  • Chargers
  • Pet essentials

Common questions

First-night box questions

What should go in a first-night box?+

Medication, toiletries, a change of clothes, chargers, bedding, a few kitchen basics, simple tools, cleaning supplies and anything your kids or pets need for the first night.

Should the first-night box go on the moving truck?+

It’s best kept in your own car so it’s with you the moment you arrive.
