What white-glove means

White-glove moving is a full-service move with more care and fewer decisions left to you. It's popular for larger homes, fine furniture and busy families who want to walk into a finished home.

Typically included

  • Full packing with extra protection for fragile and valuable items
  • Furniture disassembly, reassembly and placement to your floor plan
  • Unpacking and setup of the rooms you choose
  • Removal of packing materials

Protecting the home

Floors, door frames, banisters and walls get protection at both homes, and pieces are carried—never dragged.

Plan it together

Walk us through the home and point out what matters most. The details go into your written proposal so expectations are clear.

Common questions

White-glove moving questions

What's included in a white-glove move?+

Full packing, extra protection, furniture placement, unpacking of the rooms you choose and removal of packing materials. Your written proposal spells out the details.

Is white-glove moving only for large homes?+

No. Any move where you want every step handled with extra care can be white-glove.

Can you set up furniture where I want it?+

Yes. Share a simple floor plan and the crew places and reassembles furniture accordingly.
