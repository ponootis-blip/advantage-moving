Moving smart in Texas

A good mover is happy to be checked.

Texas law sets clear rules for household movers. Here’s how to check any company in five minutes—starting with us.

  1. 1

    Look up the TxDMV number

    Every household mover in Texas must hold an active TxDMV certificate. Ours is #000572890B—enter it in the state’s Truck Stop database and confirm it says “Active.”

  2. 2

    Get the proposal in writing

    Texas movers must give you a written proposal showing the most you could be required to pay—before anything is loaded—plus the state’s rights and responsibilities sheet.

  3. 3

    Watch for marked trucks

    Licensed movers display their company name and TxDMV number on both sides of the truck. Unmarked rental trucks and pressure to sign quickly are red flags.
