What this calculator does—and doesn't—do

It gives a planning range based on typical crew sizes and hours for each home size, plus mileage for moves elsewhere in Texas and common add-ons. It's a starting point for your budget, not a binding quote.

What makes a real quote different

Your written proposal accounts for what a calculator can't see: stairs and elevators, the distance from the truck to the door, how much is packed, large or fragile items and your date. See how moving quotes work in Texas.

Ways to keep costs down

  • Declutter before the move—less to pack and carry
  • Pack non-fragile rooms yourself and leave the kitchen to the crew
  • Have everything packed and ready before the crew arrives
  • Reserve elevators and parking so no time is lost on the day
  • Choose a mid-month, mid-week date if you have flexibility

Common questions

Moving cost questions

Is the calculator result a quote?+

No. It's a planning range. Your written estimate is based on the details of your move, and Texas movers must give you a written proposal before loading.

What affects the cost of a move most?+

The amount you're moving, access at both homes, distance, extra services like packing, and timing.

How can I lower my moving cost?+

Declutter, pack what you can, have everything ready on time, and choose a mid-month weekday if possible.
