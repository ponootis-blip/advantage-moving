When you need furniture moved but aren't moving

Some of the heaviest lifting happens without a change of address. Common reasons:

  • New flooring or carpet installation
  • Kitchen, bathroom or room remodels
  • Staging a home for sale
  • Moving a piano, armoire or bed upstairs or downstairs

Protecting the house

Doorways, stair rails and floors take the most damage when heavy pieces move inside a home. We pad furniture, protect floors and corners, and take pieces apart when that's safer.

Remodels and flooring

Coordinate with your contractor on timing: furniture usually needs to come out of the room the day before work starts and go back once floors have cured. Short-term storage can help if rooms will be out of action for a while—see storage between homes.

Staging for sale

Realtors and homeowners often need furniture removed, rearranged or brought in for listing photos. Tell us the plan and the deadline.
