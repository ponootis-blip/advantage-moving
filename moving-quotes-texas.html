How moving quotes work in Texas.

Binding or not-to-exceed, what changes the price, and why stairs and parking matter—explained by a licensed Texas mover.

What Texas requires

Licensed Texas movers must give you a written proposal before loading anything. It's either binding—the exact price of the move—or not-to-exceed—the most you can be charged. It should also say when payment is due and which payment methods are accepted. Source: TxDMV ↗.

What changes the price of a move

  • How much is moving. More furniture and boxes means more time, and sometimes a bigger crew or truck.
  • Access at both homes. Stairs, elevators, long walks from the truck and tight doorways all take time.
  • Distance. Local moves are mostly about time; Texas moves add the drive.
  • Extra services. Packing, disassembly and specialty items such as pianos.
  • Timing. End-of-month and summer dates are the busiest.

Do movers charge extra for stairs?

Stairs take more time and effort, so they usually affect the price—either as a line item or through the time the move takes. The fair way to handle it is up front: tell your mover about every staircase, elevator and long carry at both addresses so the written proposal reflects the real job.

How to get an accurate quote

Be specific. List large furniture, mention anything heavy or fragile, share photos of tricky access and give both full addresses. Surprises on moving day are what make final prices differ from expectations.

Comparing quotes side by side

Is each quote binding or not-to-exceed? Does it include packing materials, disassembly and the drive? Is the mover's TxDMV certificate active? The lowest number isn't the best deal if it leaves things out. For more, see 10 questions to ask a mover.

Common questions

Moving quote questions

What's the difference between a binding and a not-to-exceed proposal?+

A binding proposal states the exact price. A not-to-exceed proposal states the maximum you can be charged. Texas movers must give you one in writing before loading.

Do movers charge extra for stairs?+

Stairs add time and effort, so they usually affect the price. Mention every staircase and elevator at both addresses so your written proposal reflects the real job.

Why do moving quotes vary so much?+

Movers make different assumptions about time, crew size and access. The more detail you share, the closer quotes get to each other—and to the final price.

Is the ballpark estimator on this site a quote?+

No. It gives a planning range only. Your personal written estimate is based on the details of your move.
