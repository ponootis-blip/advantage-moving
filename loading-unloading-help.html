Why loading matters more than driving

Most damage in a do-it-yourself move happens inside the truck: furniture shifts, boxes crush and unpadded pieces rub together on the highway. A well-loaded truck uses every inch, keeps heavy items low and locks the load in place.

Rental trucks and moving containers

Whether it's a rental truck or a portable container, the job is the same: heavy and square items first, tall pieces tied off, boxes stacked by weight and every gap filled. Tell us the size of the truck or container so we bring the right crew.

Unloading at the other end

We can unload and place furniture and boxes in the right rooms, and reassemble pieces we took apart.

Before the crew arrives

  • Have the truck or container parked as close to the door as possible
  • Pack and seal boxes, and label them by room
  • Have pads, straps and a dolly in the truck if the rental includes them
