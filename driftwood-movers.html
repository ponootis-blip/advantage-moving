Moving in Driftwood

Driftwood is a Hill Country community in Hays County, spread along FM 1826 and FM 150 between Austin, Dripping Springs and Buda. Properties tend to be large, private and set well back from the road.

Gates, gravel and grades

Narrow gates, gravel or caliche drives and steep grades decide what size truck can reach the house. Photos of the drive and the turnaround help us plan.

Weather and unpaved drives

Heavy rain can soften unpaved roads. Keep a backup parking spot in mind and talk to us if the forecast turns.

Outdoor living

Patio furniture, outdoor kitchens, planters and equipment are part of most Driftwood moves. Include them in your quote request.

Driftwood moving questions

Do you serve Driftwood?+

Yes. Driftwood is within our 30-mile service area, between Austin, Buda and Dripping Springs.

What if a big truck can't reach the house?+

Tell us before moving day. Access determines the truck, crew and timing, and it's far easier to plan for than to discover on the day.

Do you move outdoor furniture and equipment?+

Yes. List outdoor pieces when you request your quote so they're included.
