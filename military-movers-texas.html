Moving on orders within Texas

Military moves come with deadlines, paperwork and a lot of uncertainty. When your move stays inside Texas, we can help.

Personally procured moves (PPM)

With a PPM, you arrange your own move and are reimbursed under military rules. Before you book, confirm what your transportation office requires—such as weight tickets, receipts and the type of paperwork you'll need from the mover—so nothing is missed.

Local moves

Moving off base, between homes near an installation, or into a new rental? Local moves get the same full-service care as any other.

Our limits

We move households within Texas only. For moves out of state, your transportation office can explain your options.

Before you book

Check with your transportation office.

  • Your move type (PPM or other)
  • Required receipts and weight tickets
  • Report and move dates
  • Both addresses
  • Storage needs
